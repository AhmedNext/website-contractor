# U.S. keyword and competition review

Checked October 12, 2026. This is a research plan for the 20 existing tool routes, not a traffic forecast. No new calculator routes were created.

## Data and limitations

Anonymous seodata.dev lookups requested `country=us`; raw results and cache timestamps are in [keyword-demand.json](keyword-demand.json). The provider states that its data originates from the Google Ads API; it was not independently cross-checked in a Google Ads account. Reported volume is for the selected term and close variants, not a guaranteed number of visitors to our site. Cache dates range from May to October 2026. Thirteen terms returned usable volume data. Seven HTTP 404 / unavailable results are treated as unknown demand, even though their payload included zero.

Google explains [average monthly searches and advertising competition](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/3022575?hl=en). Advertising competition is not organic SEO difficulty. Do not add overlapping broad and long-tail volumes. Search-result research below is qualitative and not a geo-locked Google US rank report. Candidate secondary terms have not all received separate volume checks.

## Competitors actually found

- [Calculator.net concrete](https://www.calculator.net/concrete-calculator.html), [roofing](https://www.calculator.net/roofing-calculator.html) and [square footage](https://www.calculator.net/square-footage-calculator.html): established broad quantity tools.
- [Wall Framing Calculator](https://www.wallframingcalculator.com/): already offers live 2D/3D views. Visuals alone are not a unique ranking advantage.
- [USG wallboard estimator](https://assemblies-tools.usg.com/content/usgcom/en/resource-center/tools/sheetrockestimator.html): manufacturer-backed material estimation.
- [CalculateGravel](https://calculategravel.com/): dedicated yards, tons and cost tool.
- [ContractorMargin roofing](https://contractormargin.com/roofing): dedicated job-profit competitor.
- [ServiceTitan plumbing labor rate](https://www.servicetitan.com/tools/plumbing-labor-rate-calculator), [Operator](https://www.nowoperator.com/tools/plumbing-labor-rate-calculator) and [Fieldified](https://fieldified.com/tools/plumbing-labor-rate-calculator): this trade keyword is already served by specialist software companies.
- [TrustPro tools](https://www.trustpro.io/free-tools) and [Contractor Invoice Generator](https://www.contractorinvoicegenerator.com/): no-signup documents already exist.
- [SheetSteps construction tracker](https://www.sheetsteps.com/templates/construction-job-cost-tracker): spreadsheet intent competes with our browser workflow.
- [SubWaiver](https://subwaiver.com/) and official state forms: lien-document competition requires accuracy, not just attractive output. Competitor compliance claims were not adopted as verified facts.

## What to do before building another tool

1. Improve the existing concrete, aggregate, drywall and framing pages around specific buying decisions; examples and comparison tables were added in this change. Keep net quantities separate from rounded orders.
2. Let each route own its intent: roofing takeoff is materials; roofing profit is business costs; change-order builder produces a document; change-order profit measures the change. Link between them rather than publishing near-duplicates.
3. Verify narrower terms with [Google Keyword Planner](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7337243?hl=en), United States, English, Google network, last 12 months. Export source dates and inspect seasonality. Account setup is required; no account or paid service was created here.
4. In Search Console, inspect impressions, queries, selected canonical and indexing for each route. Indexability is not proof of actual indexing. Public site-search results are not a reliable inventory.
5. Review 28 days of real query impressions after Google starts showing the pages. Prioritize useful missing explanations and conversions before adding routes. Do not promise page-one rankings, a million visits, or AdSense income from these volumes.
