# Search-intent research — October 11, 2026

Live qualitative search inspected contractor markup/margin and retainage cash-flow calculator results. No search-volume, CPC, difficulty, or traffic figures were available or inferred.

Observed competitor features: retainage withheld by invoice, cash timing, payment delay, advance credit, and a focus on the financing gap of otherwise profitable jobs. Examples inspected:
- https://profoundestimates.com/tools/cash-flow-retainage-calculator/
- https://buildfigure.org/retainage-cash-flow-calculator
- https://www.winkscope.com/tools/retainage-calculator

Product differentiation: six connected tools; explicit overhead and expected contingency expense; 6/12-month editable invoice and expense schedules; out-of-horizon collections; unapplied advances; clear operating assumptions. These are feature observations, not claims about competitors' traffic or accuracy.

Intent hypotheses: “contractor job profit calculator” signals bid review; “contractor break even jobs” signals monthly capacity planning; “contractor cash flow retainage calculator” signals payment-timing risk; “labor charge out rate calculator” signals wage-to-price conversion; “markup vs margin contractor” signals denominator confusion; “change order profit calculator” signals scope pricing. Pages use these natural phrases without thin duplicate variations.
